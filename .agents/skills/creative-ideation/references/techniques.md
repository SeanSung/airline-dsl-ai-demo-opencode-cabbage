# 创意技法库

> 108 个创意技法，按 13 个类别组织。每个技法的机制在一句话里讲完。
> 用法：按目标选 3–4 个为一批；卡壳时优先换类别（从一个类别跳到另一个）
> 而不是在同一类别里硬撑。`good_for` 标签提示该技法擅长的场景：
> novel=找新意 · unstuck=破局 · diagnosis=诊断问题 · strategy=战略 ·
> feature=产品功能 · planning=规划 · personal=个人决策。

## 协作（collaborative）

- **Yes And Building** — Never negate; each person opens with "Yes, and..." and adds to the last idea, stacking a chain of accepted additions
  _适用：novel / unstuck / planning_
- **Brain Writing Round Robin** — Everyone writes ideas silently, then passes their sheet; you build on whatever lands in front of you, round after round
  _适用：novel / feature_
- **Random Stimulation** — Pull a random word or image and force a link to the problem: "how does THIS spark a solution?"
  _适用：unstuck / novel_
- **Role Playing** — Each person speaks as a different stakeholder, voicing what that role wants, fears, and would demand of the idea
  _适用：strategy / personal / feature_
- **Ideation Relay Race** — 30-second turns, no pausing: add one idea, slap it to the next person, keep the baton moving before anyone overthinks
  _适用：unstuck_
- **Idea Hot Potato** — One idea gets tossed around the circle; each catcher must mutate it in 10 seconds before passing, no repeats allowed
  _适用：unstuck_
- **Steal And Upgrade** — Pick a neighbor's idea you envy, claim it out loud, then make it visibly better before handing it back improved
  _适用：novel / unstuck_
- **Fold The Paper** — Each person adds one line to a hidden drawing or sentence, sees only the previous fragment, then unfold the surreal whole
  _适用：unstuck / novel_

## 创意（creative）

- **What If Scenarios** — Detonate one constraint at a time — unlimited budget, opposite is true, problem vanished — and chase what rushes in
  _适用：novel / strategy / unstuck_
- **Analogical Thinking** — Ask 'this is like what?' and steal the solution pattern from the domain that answers
  _适用：feature / novel / diagnosis_
- **First Principles Thinking** — Strip every assumption to bedrock facts, then rebuild the solution from scratch on truth alone
  _适用：feature / novel / diagnosis / strategy_
- **Forced Relationships** — Grab two unrelated things at random and force a bridge between them until an idea falls out
  _适用：novel / unstuck_
- **Time Shifting** — Solve the problem as a 1900s artisan, then a 2150 colonist — harvest the era-bound constraints and tricks
  _适用：novel / unstuck_
- **Metaphor Mapping** — Declare the problem IS a chosen metaphor, extend the metaphor fully, map each part back to find insights
  _适用：novel / diagnosis_
- **Cross-Pollination** — Ask how a wildly different industry — casinos, ERs, beekeeping — would crack this, then adapt their move
  _适用：novel / feature / strategy_
- **Concept Blending** — Fuse two concepts into one new hybrid category and name what the merger becomes, not just combines
  _适用：novel_
- **Reverse Brainstorming** — Generate problems instead of solutions — 'how could we make this fail?' — then mine each for its inverse
  _适用：diagnosis / feature / unstuck_
- **Sensory Exploration** — Interrogate the idea through each sense — its taste, smell, sound, texture — to surface non-analytical angles
  _适用：novel / unstuck_

## 深挖（deep）

- **Five Whys** — Ask "why?" five times in a chain, each answer feeding the next, until you hit the root cause beneath the symptom
  _适用：diagnosis_
- **Provocation Technique** — State something deliberately absurd, then mine it: "how could this be useful?" Extract the usable principle hiding inside
  _适用：unstuck / novel_
- **Assumption Reversal** — List every assumption baked into the problem, flip each to its opposite, then rebuild a solution on the inverted foundation
  _适用：novel / diagnosis / strategy_
- **Question Storming** — Generate only questions about the problem, zero answers allowed, until the real problem worth solving comes into focus
  _适用：diagnosis / strategy / unstuck_
- **Constraint Mapping** — Map every constraint, sort real from imagined, then attack each: dissolve it, route around it, or turn it into an asset
  _适用：feature / strategy / diagnosis_
- **Failure Analysis** — Dissect a relevant failure: what broke, why it broke, what lesson it leaves, and how to apply that wisdom here
  _适用：diagnosis / strategy / feature_
- **Emergent Thinking** — Stop forcing a solution; watch what patterns the system keeps producing and name what's trying to emerge on its own
  _适用：strategy / novel_
- **Causal Loop Mapping** — Diagram the feedback loops linking causes and effects, find the reinforcing and balancing cycles, and target the leverage point
  _适用：diagnosis / strategy_
- **Morphological Analysis** — List the problem's independent parameters, generate options for each, then combine across them to surface untried configurations
  _适用：feature / novel / planning_
- **Laddering** — Ask 'and what would that give you?' up the chain until you reach the real underlying need, then ideate fresh at that level
  _适用：personal / strategy / diagnosis_
- **TRIZ Contradiction** — Name the core contradiction (what only improves by making something else worse), then brainstorm ways to win both instead of trading off
  _适用：feature / novel / diagnosis_
- **Fishbone Diagram** — Branch the problem's spine into cause categories (people, process, tools, environment) and mine each bone for contributing causes
  _适用：diagnosis_
- **Build on What Works** — Name what's already succeeding and why, then ideate how to amplify and extend it instead of fixing what's broken
  _适用：personal / strategy_

## 内观与愉悦（introspective_delight）

- **Inner Child Conference** — Answer as your 7-year-old self: ask naive 'why why why' questions, chase wonder, ban every boring adult thought
  _适用：personal / unstuck_
- **Shadow Work Mining** — Name what you're avoiding, resisting, or scared of about this — then dig there for the buried insight
  _适用：personal / diagnosis_
- **Values Archaeology** — Keep asking 'why do I care?' until you hit bedrock: the non-negotiable value secretly steering the choice
  _适用：personal / strategy_
- **Future Self Interview** — Interview your wise 80-year-old self about this problem and write down the advice they give you
  _适用：personal_
- **Body Wisdom Dialogue** — Scan for the tension, flutter, or gut pull each option triggers; let the body's yes/no drive the ideas
  _适用：personal_
- **Permission Giving** — Write yourself an explicit permission slip to think the forbidden, impossible thought — then think it out loud
  _适用：personal / unstuck_
- **Secret Wish Confession** — Whisper the embarrassing thing you secretly want here but won't admit, then build the idea honoring it
  _适用：personal_
- **Mood Weather Report** — Name the inner weather right now (fog, storm, sun) and let that exact emotional climate generate the ideas
  _适用：personal / unstuck_

## 结构化（structured）

- **SCAMPER Method** — Run your idea through seven lenses: Substitute, Combine, Adapt, Modify, Put-to-other-use, Eliminate, Reverse
  _适用：feature / novel_
- **Six Thinking Hats** — Examine the problem six ways one at a time: facts, feelings, benefits, risks, new ideas, process
  _适用：strategy / diagnosis / planning / personal_
- **Decision Tree Mapping** — Chart every choice point and the paths it forks into, following each branch to its outcome and risk
  _适用：planning / strategy / diagnosis_
- **Solution Matrix** — Grid problem variables against solution approaches, score every cell, hunt the best pairings and empty gaps
  _适用：feature / planning_
- **Trait Transfer** — Name what makes an unrelated success work, then graft those winning traits onto your own problem
  _适用：novel / feature_
- **Lotus Blossom** — Put the theme at the center of a 3x3 grid, fill the 8 cells around it, then promote each of those to the center of its own new 3x3
  _适用：feature / planning / novel_
- **Worst Possible Idea** — Deliberately generate the most terrible solutions you can, then flip each into what it teaches you to do right
  _适用：unstuck / novel_
- **Disney Method** — Cycle the idea through three rooms: Dreamer (anything goes), Realist (how we'd build it), Critic (what breaks)
  _适用：feature / strategy / planning_
- **Starbursting** — Interrogate the idea with only questions — who, what, where, when, why, how — exhaust each before answering any
  _适用：feature / planning / diagnosis_
- **Mind Mapping** — Branch the central topic outward, each node spawning children; follow tangents wherever they pull and let the web sprawl
  _适用：planning / novel / feature_
- **Crazy 8s** — Eight ideas in eight minutes, one per box, no editing — speed outruns your inner critic
  _适用：feature / novel / unstuck_
- **How Might We** — Reframe the problem as a batch of 'How might we...' opportunity questions first, then ideate against the sharpest one
  _适用：feature / novel / strategy / diagnosis_
- **Job to Be Done** — Ask what the user is really hiring this to do, then ideate around that underlying job, not the feature you assumed
  _适用：feature / strategy / novel_
- **Empathy Map** — Map what the user says, thinks, does, and feels around the problem, then mine each quadrant for the unmet need
  _适用：feature / personal_
- **Backcasting** — Fix the finished future in vivid detail, then work backward step by step to the one move you'd have to make first
  _适用：strategy / planning / novel_

## 戏剧化（theatrical）

- **Time Travel Talk Show** — Host a talk show interviewing your past, present, and future selves to mine each era for advice on the problem
  _适用：novel / personal_
- **Alien Anthropologist** — Become a baffled alien studying the problem and narrate aloud what seems strange, arbitrary, or insane about it
  _适用：diagnosis / unstuck / strategy_
- **Dream Fusion Laboratory** — Voice the impossible fantasy solution first, then reverse-engineer the bridging steps back to reality
  _适用：novel / unstuck_
- **Emotion Orchestra** — Run a separate ideation round led by each emotion (rage, joy, fear, hope), then harmonize their conflicting ideas
  _适用：personal / strategy_
- **Parallel Universe Cafe** — Rewrite one fundamental rule of reality (physics, economics, social norms) and solve the problem under those laws
  _适用：novel / unstuck_
- **Persona Journey** — Embody an archetype and solve the problem in-character, naming what that persona sees that you normally miss
  _适用：feature / strategy_
- **Devil's Advocate Courtroom** — Stage a trial: prosecute the idea, defend it, then deliver the jury verdict, each role argued fully in character
  _适用：strategy / diagnosis_

## 狂野（wild）

- **Chaos Engineering** — Deliberately break your idea every way it could fail, then rebuild only the parts that survive the wreckage
  _适用：feature / diagnosis / strategy_
- **Guerrilla Gardening Ideas** — Plant your solution in the least expected place and let it grow underground until it surprises everyone
  _适用：strategy / unstuck_
- **Pirate Code Brainstorm** — Steal the best bits from anywhere, remix without asking permission, grab what works and run
  _适用：novel / unstuck_
- **Zombie Apocalypse Planning** — Society just collapsed — strip your idea to only what survives with no power, no rules, no backup
  _适用：feature / strategy / unstuck_
- **Drunk History Retelling** — Explain it like you're three drinks in: no filter, no jargon, just the raw stupid-simple truth
  _适用：unstuck / diagnosis_
- **Anti-Solution** — Brainstorm how to make the problem spectacularly worse, then invert every sabotage into a fix
  _适用：diagnosis / unstuck_
- **Elemental Forces** — Let fire, water, earth, and air each sculpt your idea their own brutal way and see what survives
  _适用：novel / unstuck_

## 仿生（biomimetic）

- **Nature's Solutions** — Name an organism that already solved your problem, then copy its mechanism into your design
  _适用：feature / novel_
- **Ecosystem Thinking** — Map your problem as an ecosystem: who eats whom, who partners, what decays, what fills the gaps
  _适用：strategy / diagnosis_
- **Evolutionary Pressure** — Spawn many ugly variants, apply a brutal selection rule, breed the survivors, repeat until it adapts
  _适用：feature / novel_
- **Predator & Prey** — Pick a threat to your idea, then design the defense, camouflage, or escape an animal would evolve against it
  _适用：strategy / feature_
- **Metamorphosis Stages** — Force your idea through egg, larva, pupa, adult: a radically different form and purpose at each life stage
  _适用：novel / strategy_
- **Swarm Logic** — Forbid the master plan: solve it with dumb local rules each agent follows so order emerges from the bottom up
  _适用：feature / strategy_

## 量子隐喻（quantum）

- **Observer Effect** — Ask how the act of watching, measuring, or shipping this idea changes the very thing you're trying to capture
  _适用：strategy / diagnosis_
- **Entanglement Thinking** — Pair two distant parts of the problem and insist a change in one instantly flips the other — surface the hidden linkage
  _适用：diagnosis / strategy_
- **Superposition Collapse** — Hold all rival solutions alive at once, then name the one constraint that collapses them to a single winner
  _适用：strategy / diagnosis_
- **Relativity Frame Shift** — Re-run the idea from a wildly different observer's reference frame — the slow user, the rival, future-you — and see what warps
  _适用：strategy / novel_
- **Field Lines** — Treat the goal as a charge and map the invisible forces pulling every stakeholder toward or away from it
  _适用：strategy_
- **Quantum Tunneling** — Assume the idea can pass straight through the 'impossible' barrier instead of over it — what's on the other side, reached cheaply
  _适用：unstuck / novel_

## 文化（cultural）

- **Indigenous Wisdom** — Ask how an indigenous or traditional knowledge system would approach this — name the culture, channel its ancestral problem-solving
  _适用：personal / strategy / novel_
- **Fusion Cuisine** — Pick two unrelated cultures and force-blend their approaches; harvest the hybrid that neither alone would invent
  _适用：novel_
- **Ritual Innovation** — Redesign the idea as a ceremony — define the threshold, the gestures, the transformation participants undergo
  _适用：novel / personal_
- **Mythic Frameworks** — Map the problem onto a myth: name the archetypes, find the parallel tale, let its structure dictate the resolution
  _适用：strategy / personal / novel_
- **Proverb Mining** — Collect proverbs from many cultures on this theme, then build the solution from the one that clashes hardest with your assumptions
  _适用：personal / strategy_
- **Ancestor Council** — Convene three ancestors or elders from different traditions, voice each one's verdict on your idea, reconcile their disagreement
  _适用：personal / strategy_
- **Trickster's Gambit** — Channel the trickster figure — coyote, Anansi, Loki — and solve it by cheating, inverting, or breaking the sacred rule
  _适用：unstuck / strategy_

## 荒诞（absurdist）

- **Villain's Monologue** — Pitch your problem as an evil mastermind gloating about their scheme; the diabolical plan reveals the real solution
  _适用：diagnosis / strategy / unstuck_
- **Explain It to a Golden Retriever** — Re-pitch the idea to an excitable dog who only cares about treats, balls, and naps; keep only what survives
  _适用：unstuck / diagnosis / feature_
- **Infomercial at 3AM** — Sell your half-baked idea as a desperate late-night infomercial: 'But wait, there's more!' until features fall out
  _适用：strategy / novel_
- **Drunk Uncle at Thanksgiving** — Have your loudest, least-filtered relative rant about the problem; mine the unhinged hot takes for buried truth
  _适用：unstuck / diagnosis_
- **Cursed Genie** — Make a wish, then let a malicious genie grant it in the most technically-correct disastrous way; patch each loophole
  _适用：diagnosis / feature_
- **Three Rounds of Stupid** — Round 1 absurd ideas, Round 2 make each MORE absurd, Round 3 find the smallest serious thing hiding in the silliest
  _适用：unstuck / novel_

## 约束（constraint）

- **Kill the Crown Jewel** — Delete the single best, most beloved feature — now redesign the whole thing to win without it
  _适用：feature / strategy / unstuck_
- **1000x Budget** — Pretend money, time, and people are infinite — design the absurd version, then mine it for ideas you can actually steal
  _适用：novel / strategy_
- **Ship in 60 Minutes** — You launch in one hour with what's already on hand — name what you cut, fake, or borrow to make it real
  _适用：feature / planning / unstuck_
- **The $0 Mandate** — Achieve the goal spending literally nothing — no tools, hires, or ads; only people, favors, and what you own
  _适用：planning / strategy / feature_
- **One Feature Only** — You may keep exactly ONE capability and nothing else — pick it, then make that single thing unbelievably good
  _适用：feature / strategy_
- **Crank the Dial to 11** — Pick one dimension and exaggerate it to a ludicrous extreme — fastest, biggest, cheapest, weirdest — and see what breaks open
  _适用：novel / unstuck_
- **Constraint Roulette** — Each round draw a brutal random limit (no screens, half the team, one day) and re-solve under it; survivors become real ideas
  _适用：unstuck / feature_

## 思辨未来（speculative_future）

- **Time Horizon Ladder** — Solve the idea for 1 year out, then 10, then 100 — note what survives, breaks, or becomes absurd at each rung
  _适用：strategy / planning / novel_
- **Post-Scarcity Test** — Assume the core constraint (money, energy, time, attention) is now infinite and free — what does the idea become
  _适用：novel / strategy_
- **Utopia vs Dystopia Split-Screen** — Write the same future twice: the brochure where it went perfectly and the headline where it went horribly
  _适用：strategy / diagnosis_
- **Sci-Fi Artifact From the Future** — Describe one physical object, ad, or news clip from the world where this idea already won — reverse-engineer it
  _适用：novel / feature_
- **Emerging Tech Collision** — Force-marry your idea to a frontier tech (AGI, fusion, neural implants, gene edit) and ask what new thing is born
  _适用：novel / feature / strategy_
- **What-If-The-World-Changed Card Flip** — Draw a wild world-shift (no privacy, half population, 200-yr lifespans) and redesign the idea to fit that world
  _适用：novel / unstuck_
- **Future Anthropologist Dig** — A scholar in 2200 unearths your idea as a relic — what do they conclude it reveals about us, and what replaced it
  _适用：strategy / novel_
- **Scenario Cross** — Pick two high-impact uncertainties, cross them into four futures, and ideate the move that wins in every one
  _适用：strategy / planning_
